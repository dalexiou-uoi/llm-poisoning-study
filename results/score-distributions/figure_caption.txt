Empirical cumulative distributions of clean and poisoned influence scores within each source task. Each curve is normalized within its own task and group. Lower scores are selected first. Vertical lines denote global removal budgets of 1%, 2%, 3% and 5%, applied to the complete 30,220-row pool rather than separately within each task. All scored rows, including reference examples, are plotted. AUROC uses negative influence scores with a fixed low-score direction. Task833 has no poisoned examples; task888 has very few. The same horizontal scale is used across all four figures. Some cutoff lines nearly coincide. Scores are produced by different adapters and reference sets, so the shared scale is descriptive and does not imply calibrated comparability across poisoning fractions.
